# Ostromo for Google TV: store text

For a GitHub release, a page on staiola.studio, or later Google Play.

## Name

Ostromo

## Short description (Play: max 80 characters)

A retro sci-fi home screen for your TV: live radar, power prices, teletext.

## Description

Ostromo turns your TV into the ship's computer. A home screen in the style of the green-on-black terminals from late-seventies science fiction, drawn as vector lines with a CRT glow, scanlines and a slow phosphor flicker.

From it you open your streaming apps, and a set of screens of its own:

- **Radar**: live air traffic around your nearest big airport (Copenhagen, Malmö, Stockholm, Oslo, Berlin, Paris, London, New York, Washington, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide or Perth), with departure and arrival boards, routes and altitudes.
- **Power**: today's and tomorrow's electricity price, every quarter of an hour, and the cheapest three hours to run the dishwasher. Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Germany, London (Octopus Agile), New York City and eastern Australia.
- **Teletext**: DR Tekst-TV, SVT Text or ARD Text, live, on a real 40-column teletext grid.
- **Comms**: NTS Live radio.
- **Outposts**: webcams from research stations in Antarctica and the Arctic, and a camera on the sea floor.
- **Sentry**: a small defence-grid game.

Everything is navigated with the arrow keys and OK on the remote. A first-time setup picks the airport, power prices and teletext for where you live.

Ostromo is a fan-made homage and isn't connected to any film studio. It's free, has no ads and collects nothing.

## Data credits

Aircraft positions: adsb.fi (non-commercial use). Routes: adsbdb. Electricity: Energi Data Service, elprisetjustnu.se, hvakosterstrommen.no, Octopus Energy, NYISO, AEMO. Teletext: DR, SVT (via texttv.nu), ARD. Maps: Natural Earth.

## Notes before Google Play

- adsb.fi's open data is for personal, non-commercial use. A free app is likely fine, but ask them before listing it.
- Play needs a privacy policy URL even for an app that collects nothing: one short page on staiola.studio will do.
- Screenshots: 1920×1080, at least one; a 1280×720 TV banner.
